How margin works here

Buying options needs the full premium. Selling options and trading futures blocks margin of 12% of notional (a simplified stand-in for exchange SPAN + exposure margin). Available = cash + futures MTM − margin used. Orders that only reduce a position are never blocked. Each executed order costs ₹20; option sells also pay 0.1% STT.
